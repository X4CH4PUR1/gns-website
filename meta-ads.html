01 — Who it’s for

Who our Meta ads management is for

It fits a business that sells something people want and needs more customers than word of mouth brings in. If a new customer is worth more than it costs to win one, Meta can help you find more of them. If that math doesn’t work yet, we’ll tell you before you spend anything.

A good fit if

  • You sell something people can understand from a short video or a photo.
  • You can handle more inquiries, bookings or orders than you get today.
  • You’re ready to give a campaign its first three months to show what works.

Not a fit if

  • You need results this week. Testing takes weeks, not days.
  • Your profit per customer can’t cover the cost of winning one yet.

03 — How we run it

How we run your Meta ads

We follow the same 90-day plan as every client. The first month builds the offer, the ads, the page and the tracking. The second tests angles against each other. The third puts more budget behind what works and refreshes the creative before it wears out.

  1. 1

    Week 1—2

    Audit & offer

    We look at what you sell, who buys it and why the current version isn’t converting. Often the offer needs work before the ad account does.

  2. 2

    Week 2—4

    Build the system

    Ads, landing page, pixel and conversions API. Nothing goes live until the page is worth sending paid traffic to.

  3. 3

    Week 4—8

    Launch & learn

    Structured tests across audiences, angles and formats. We’re looking for the one angle that clearly beats the rest.

  4. 4

    Week 8—12

    Scale the winner

    Budget follows evidence. New creative goes in on a schedule, so the winning angle doesn’t wear out.

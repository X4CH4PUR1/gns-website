01 — Who it’s for

Who Google Ads management is for

It fits any business people already search for: a local service someone needs today, a clinic, or a store with a considered product. If people rarely type what you sell into Google, search ads have little to catch, and we’ll suggest starting on Meta, which creates demand instead.

A good fit if

  • People search for your service or product by name, or with “near me”.
  • A new customer is worth more than it costs to win one.
  • You can answer calls and forms quickly when leads come in.
  • You want PPC management for a local business that someone reads every week.

Not a fit if

  • Nobody searches for what you sell yet. That’s a job for Meta.
  • You want to pay for clicks without fixing the page they land on.
  • You need the phone ringing tomorrow. Testing takes weeks.

Every kind of business can run search ads. See the industries we work with.

03 — How we run it

How we run your Google Ads

Every account follows our 90-day plan. We start with the offer and the searches that matter, build the campaigns, page and tracking, test what brings real leads, then move budget to the keywords and ads that earn it. Lead quality, not click volume, decides what stays.

  1. 1

    Week 1—2

    Audit & offer

    We look at what you sell, what people search for and, if you’ve run Google Ads before, where the old budget went.

  2. 2

    Week 2—4

    Build the system

    Keyword structure, ads, landing page, conversion and call tracking. Nothing goes live until the page is worth the click.

  3. 3

    Week 4—8

    Launch & learn

    Structured tests on keywords, ads and offers. Search terms and negatives are reviewed every week.

  4. 4

    Week 8—12

    Scale the winner

    Budget follows evidence: more behind the searches that bring customers, less behind the ones that don’t.

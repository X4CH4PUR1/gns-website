01 — Who it’s for

Who business automation services are for

They suit a business where the same steps follow every lead, sale or job, and someone does them by hand when they remember. If leads wait hours for a reply or reviews never get asked for, automation fixes the habit, not just the task. A business with a few jobs a month may not need it.

A good fit if

  • Leads arrive from ads, forms and calls faster than anyone can reply.
  • You copy the same details between your inbox, a spreadsheet and your CRM.
  • You finish jobs but rarely ask for the review.
  • You want workflow automation for a small business without hiring someone to run it.

Not a fit if

  • Your process changes every week. Automate it once it’s settled.
  • There’s no task that repeats often enough to be worth automating.

Automation works for every kind of business with repeat steps. See the industries we work with.

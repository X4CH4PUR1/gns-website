01 — Who it’s for

When AI agents for small business make sense

They make sense when inquiries arrive faster than you can answer them, or at hours when nobody is working. If the same questions come in every day and a slow reply costs you bookings, an AI agent is worth a look. If you get a few inquiries a week, a person is still the better answer.

A good fit if

  • You get the same questions about price, availability and process every day.
  • Leads come in by chat, DM or web form outside working hours.
  • You want an AI receptionist that books appointments, not one that only collects names.
  • Your bookings run through a calendar or booking tool the agent can work with.

Not a fit if

  • Most of your sales need a long, personal conversation from the first message.
  • You get only a handful of inquiries a week.
  • You want it to promise or quote things nobody has approved.

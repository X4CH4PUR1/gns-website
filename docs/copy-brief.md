Read this before writing any copy on the site. These facts come from the owners. Never add a fact that isn't here or already on the site.

## What we sell
- Meta & Instagram ads, Google Search & Shopping ads, TikTok ads, landing pages and websites, AI agents, automation, app builds.
- Ad creative and video editing are part of the work. 
- Prices: only as published on pricing.html (USD). Prices are deliberately exact (1,618, not 1,600): never round them. AI agent, automation and app build are "From" prices, and are add-ons to Crown only.
- Ad spend is paid by the client straight to the platforms. We never mark it up or route it through us.
- Minimum term and contract terms: only as stated on pricing.html.

## Who we write for
- Owners of any business, in any industry, who want more customers from ads. We work with every kind of business: never write that we specialize in, focus on or only serve one industry or niche.
- They are busy and wary of agencies; many have paid for vague reports before. They want to know what we do, what it costs, who does the work, and whether it will pay back.

## Proof
- No client results yet. Say so plainly where it matters ("we're taking our first clients"). Never invent results, figures, testimonials, reviews, client names or logos.
- Real work to point to: logimotors.com (designed and built by us) and gnscales.com itself. The ads on work.html are labeled illustrative concepts and must stay labeled.
- No testimonials or quotes from Logimotors or Need for Cars.

## Voice
- Plain, direct, honest. "We" for GN Scales, "you" for the reader. Short sentences, one idea per paragraph.
- Say the uncomfortable part: when we're not a fit, that results aren't guaranteed, what it really costs including ad spend.
- Concrete over clever: name the platform, the step, the deliverable.
- Answer-first: the first sentence after any heading answers that heading.
- Never use: guaranteed, #1, best-in-class, skyrocket, explode, unlock, game-changer, synergy, cutting-edge, "in today's digital landscape", exclamation marks, emojis, statistics without a source.
- Spelling: US English everywhere (math, optimize, color, analyze, center, tires).
- Call to action: "Book a strategy call" linking to contact.html. The call is free. Never state a fixed length (no "20 minutes", no "30 minutes"): it takes as long as the business needs.

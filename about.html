About GN ScalesTwo marketers. No middlemen.

GN Scales is Nick and George Lomidze. We plan, make and run your marketing ourselves — strategy, creative and paid ads — so the people you talk to are the people doing the work.

01 — Our story

Why we started GN Scales

We started GN Scales because we kept seeing the same thing: businesses paying one agency for ads, a freelancer for creative and someone else for the website — and nobody owning the result.

When ads stop working in a setup like that, everyone points at someone else. The agency blames the creative, the designer blames the targeting, and you pay for all of it while the number stays flat.

So we built GN Scales to own the whole thing. We work out your offer, make the ads, build the landing page and run the campaigns — and when something underperforms, we fix it ourselves, that same week.

We’re a young agency and we say so. We’re taking on our first five clients and choosing them carefully: two people can only do this properly for five businesses at once, and we’d rather do five brilliantly than twenty badly.

02 — The team

The people doing your marketing

No account managers, no junior staff, nobody passing messages. When you talk to GN Scales, you talk to Nick or George.

03 — How we work

Four promises we make every client

01

Your money stays yours

Ad spend goes from your card to the platform. We never route it, hold it, or take a percentage of it. Our incentive is your result, not your budget size.

02

You own everything we make

Landing pages, creative, ad accounts, tracking. All built in your accounts and handed over in full. Leaving should cost you nothing but the notice period.

03

We say no when it is a no

If your margin can’t carry paid ads yet, or the real problem is operations rather than demand, we’ll tell you on the first call instead of selling you a retainer.

04

No invented proof

We don’t have case studies yet, and we won’t dress up a forecast as one. When we have real results, you’ll see real numbers with the client’s name on them.

About

Design craft, pointed at growth

Our story

GN Scales started with a simple observation: most performance marketing agencies are good at buying media and mediocre at design, while most design studios can't touch a media account. Our founders spent years designing for automotive and industrial B2B companies — work where precision and craft aren't optional — before turning that discipline toward paid media.

We started GN Scales to close that gap: campaigns backed by real design work, not template graphics bolted onto a media plan. We're an early-stage agency, currently building toward our first 10 clients, and we're building deliberately — taking on partners we can genuinely serve well rather than scaling for its own sake.

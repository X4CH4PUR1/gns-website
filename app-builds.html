01 — Who it’s for

When a custom app for small business makes sense

It makes sense when off-the-shelf software forces your team into workarounds: spreadsheets, copying and pasting, three tools for one job. If a ready-made tool already fits how you work, use it. It’s cheaper, and we’ll tell you so. A custom app is worth building when your process is your advantage.

A good fit if

  • Your team runs the business on spreadsheets and workarounds.
  • You have a process no off-the-shelf tool handles well.
  • Your customers would book, order or track something more easily in an app.
  • You plan to keep and grow the tool for years.

Not a fit if

  • A ready-made tool already does most of what you need.
  • You’re still working out how the process should run.

02 — What’s included

What’s included in an app build

Everything from working out what the app must do to keeping it running after launch. We map your process, design the screens, build and test the app, launch it with your team and look after it with monthly care. You deal with the same two people from start to finish.

  • Process mapping and a written scope before any build
  • Screen design for phone, desktop or both
  • The build and its testing
  • Launch with your team or your customers
  • Monthly care: fixes, updates and small changes
  • Built by the same two people you already talk to

Smaller jobs may not need an app at all: automation often removes the busywork for less.

01 — Why GN Scales

Most agencies only run the ads. We make them too.

A typical agency takes the photos and videos you already have, loads them into an ad account and adjusts the targeting. When results stall, changing the audience is all they can do — the ad itself isn’t theirs to fix.

We work the other way round. We write your offer, make the ads and build the landing page ourselves, then run the campaigns against them. When something stops working, we fix the part that actually broke — the offer, the ad or the page — not just the targeting.

03 — Process

Your first 90 days, step by step

  1. 1

    Week 1—2

    Audit & offer

    We look hard at what you sell and how you sell it. Half the time the offer is the problem, not the ad account.

  2. 2

    Week 2—4

    Build the system

    Ads, landing page, tracking. Nothing goes live until the page is worth sending your money to.

  3. 3

    Week 4—8

    Launch & learn

    Structured tests, not scattergun. We are looking for the one angle that outperforms, then we pour into it.

  4. 4

    Week 8—12

    Scale the winner

    Budget follows evidence. Creative refreshes on a cadence so the winning angle doesn’t fatigue into the ground.

03 — Process

Ninety days, four movements

  1. 1

    Week 1—2

    Audit & offer

    We pull apart what you sell and how you say it. Half the time the offer is the problem, not the ad account.

  2. 2

    Week 2—4

    Design the system

    Creative concepts, landing page, tracking. Nothing goes live until the destination is worth sending traffic to.

  3. 3

    Week 4—8

    Launch & learn

    Structured tests, not scattergun. We are looking for the one angle that outperforms, then we pour into it.

  4. 4

    Week 8—12

    Scale the winner

    Budget follows evidence. Creative refreshes on a cadence so the winning angle doesn't fatigue into the ground.
